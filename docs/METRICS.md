# Success Metrics & KPI Tracking — TokenSpend.ai

To monitor the performance of TokenSpend.ai as a developer acquisition tool, we trace the following analytics indicators:

### 1. Funnel Conversion KPIs
* **Audit Execution Rate:** Goal is **> 40%** of landing page visitors initiating an audit. (We ensure low friction by providing the prefill demo button).
* **Lead Conversion Rate (LCR):** Goal is **> 25%** of audit page executors inputting their work email to export the PDF or apply for Credex credits.
* **MQL Qualification Rate:** Goal is **> 60%** of converted leads checking the *"request subsidized credits"* box.

---

### 2. Social & Viral Metrics
* **Viral Coefficient ($K$-Factor):** Target is **$K = 0.15$**.
  * *Calculation:* $K = \text{Share Rate} \times \text{Conversion Rate of Shared Link}$.
  * *Mechanism:* Using dynamic `/api/og` tags displaying specific savings figures on Twitter/LinkedIn cards boosts CTR.
* **Average Shared Savings Display:** We track the median yearly savings shared publicly to optimize GTM marketing headers.

---

### 3. Financial Impact & Audit ROI
* **Average Audit Value Identified:** Target is **$3,500/year** in potential savings per startup.
* **Audit Engine Efficiency Ratio:** Savings found / Total reported spend. Historical targets average **25% - 40%**.
* **Credex Conversion pipeline value:** Projected customer lifetime value (LTV) of transitioned GPU serverless or API credit clients.
